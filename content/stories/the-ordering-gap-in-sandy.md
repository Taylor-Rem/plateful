Sandy reads like chain territory — the big-box corridors along State Street
and the 10600 South interchange are wall-to-wall national logos. But strip
the chains out, as [my verified catalogue](/stories/the-online-ordering-gap)
does, and Sandy still holds 50 genuinely independent restaurants.

**28 of them — 56% — have no ordering channel of their own.** Their online
orders happen through marketplace listings at
[15–30% commission](/stories/what-a-25-dollar-order-costs) or on
[DoorDash-hosted Storefront pages](/stories/doordash-storefront) (14 of the
28 — half the gap, Sandy's quiet Storefront habit). In a suburb where the
default dinner move is ordering ahead from the car, most independents can't
take that order on anything they own.

## The most-reviewed gap on the Front

Sandy's gap group is the most-established in the corridor: every one of the
28 has more than a hundred Google reviews, and they average about **1,467
each** at 4.4 stars — the highest average of any major city's gap group,
ahead of Orem's. These aren't hidden gems waiting to be discovered; they're
already discovered. What they're missing is an owned channel to convert all
that earned popularity into orders that don't pay platform rates —
especially when the chains they compete against all have apps and ordering
baked in.

## The other 22

Twenty-two Sandy independents run their own ordering, split evenly at the
top between [Toast and Olo with 6 each](/stories/what-utah-independents-actually-use).
Against the chains' in-house apps, Sandy's independents with first-party
ordering at least compete on even footing. The 28 without are competing
against Chick-fil-A's app with a phone number and someone else's platform.

## The suburban math

A Sandy independent doing $4,000 a month through the apps at 25% pays
$12,000 a year — heavily weighted, in a residential suburb, toward
[repeat customers](/stories/your-regulars-are-the-expensive-part) who live
five minutes away. Across 28 such restaurants, the annual outflow is real
money leaving a city that prides itself on supporting local.
