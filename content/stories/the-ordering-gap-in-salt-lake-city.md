Salt Lake City should be the easy case. It has the corridor's densest dining
scene, its most competitive restaurant market, and — by the numbers — the
Wasatch Front's *best* rate of direct online ordering. In
[my July catalogue](/stories/the-online-ordering-gap) of 855 independent
restaurants, 41% of Salt Lake's 148 independents have no ordering channel of
their own, against 53% corridor-wide.

And the easy case still means **61 restaurants** — the largest absolute gap
of any city on the Front.

## Who the 61 are

They are not the strugglers. Every single one of the 61 has more than a
hundred Google reviews. As a group they average about 1,270 reviews at 4.4
stars — established, loved, busy. Salt Lake's gap is concentrated where the
corridor's always is: the city's 31 independent Mexican restaurants lead the
count, with deep benches of Chinese (14), Thai (12), Vietnamese (10),
Japanese (9), and Indian (8) kitchens. The international food that makes
Salt Lake worth eating in is precisely the food you mostly can't order
directly online.

## Who the other 70 use

Salt Lake is also where restaurant ordering tech is most entrenched: 70
independents run their own first-party ordering, and Toast powers 34 of
them — nearly half, an even stronger showing than
[its 39% corridor-wide share](/stories/what-utah-independents-actually-use).
The rest scatter across Square, ChowNow, SpotOn, Olo, Clover, and a few
others. If you want to see the subscription-pricing filter at work, Salt
Lake is the cleanest example: the city with the most $$ dining rooms has
the most ordering tech, and the gap that remains sits overwhelmingly in its
budget kitchens.

## The math for one city

Put [the fee walk-through](/stories/what-a-25-dollar-order-costs) against
just this city: if only half of Salt Lake's 61 no-channel independents sell
$4,000 a month through delivery apps at 25%, that's about **$366,000 a year**
in commissions leaving the city's kitchens — for orders that are mostly
[regulars anyway](/stories/your-regulars-are-the-expensive-part).
