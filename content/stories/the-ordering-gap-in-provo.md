Provo is a college town with a college town's dining economics: tens of
thousands of students who order food on their phones as a default setting,
plus a downtown Center Street scene that's turned into one of Utah County's
best food strips. If any city's independents should be reachable online,
it's this one.

And here's the thing — after checking every restaurant against the delivery
platforms, they *are* reachable. Provo has **zero** verified zero-online
restaurants: every independent kitchen in the city sells online somehow. But
**35 of its 59 independents — 59% — don't own the channel they sell
through.** Their online ordering is a marketplace listing at
[15–30% commission](/stories/what-a-25-dollar-order-costs) or a
[DoorDash-hosted Storefront page](/stories/doordash-storefront) (14 of the
35). In the most phone-native market in Utah, the demand is fully proven —
and mostly rented.

## The best-rated gap in the corridor

Provo's signature stat survives every recheck: its gap restaurants are the
*highest-rated* of any major city's on the Wasatch Front. Thirty-four of
the 35 have more than a hundred Google reviews, averaging about 1,070 each —
at **4.5 stars**, a tick above every other big city's gap group. Led by a
dozen independent Mexican kitchens and a strong Thai bench, these are
exactly the restaurants [the corridor data](/stories/the-online-ordering-gap)
keeps finding: excellent, established, and paying rent on every order.

## The 24 who own their channel

The other side is healthier here than in Orem next door: 24 Provo
independents run their own ordering, with
[Toast](/stories/what-utah-independents-actually-use) leading at 8, then
Square, Olo, Clover, and ChowNow. Provo's gap isn't a tech desert; it's a
filter. The kitchens with $$ menus and steady volume bought ordering
software. The budget spots students actually live on largely didn't.

## The college-town math

A campus-adjacent restaurant doing $5,000 a month through the apps at 25%
pays $15,000 a year — on a customer base where
[repeat orders dominate](/stories/your-regulars-are-the-expensive-part) by
the nature of a semester. Across 35 restaurants, Provo's annual commission
outflow plausibly runs into the hundreds of thousands.
