Provo is a college town with a college town's dining economics: tens of
thousands of students who order food on their phones as a default setting,
plus a downtown Center Street scene that's turned into one of Utah County's
best food strips. If any city's independents should be reachable online,
it's this one.

**27 of Provo's 59 independent restaurants — 46% — aren't.** No first-party
online ordering: a website you can't order from, no website at all, or a
link that routes the hungriest, most phone-native customer base in Utah
straight to a delivery app charging
[15–30% commission](/stories/what-a-25-dollar-order-costs).

## The best-rated gap in the corridor

Provo's signature stat: its invisible restaurants are the *highest-rated*
of any major city's on the Wasatch Front. All 27 have more than a hundred
Google reviews, averaging about 1,057 each — at **4.5 stars**, a tick above
every other big city's gap group. Led by a dozen independent Mexican
kitchens and a strong Thai bench, these are exactly the restaurants
[the corridor data](/stories/the-online-ordering-gap) keeps finding:
excellent, established, and unreachable the moment a student opens a phone.

## The 24 who are reachable

The other side is healthier here than in Orem next door: 24 Provo
independents run their own ordering, with
[Toast](/stories/what-utah-independents-actually-use) leading at 8, then
Square, Olo, Clover, and ChowNow. Another 3 lean on DoorDash Storefront
alone — [worth reading the fine print on](/stories/doordash-storefront).
Provo's gap isn't a tech desert; it's a filter. The kitchens with $$ menus
and steady volume bought ordering software. The budget spots students
actually live on largely didn't.

## The college-town math

A campus-adjacent restaurant doing $5,000 a month through the apps at 25%
pays $15,000 a year — on a customer base where
[repeat orders dominate](/stories/your-regulars-are-the-expensive-part) by
the nature of a semester. Across 27 restaurants, Provo's annual commission
outflow plausibly runs into the hundreds of thousands.
